## What it is

`main.py` draws a single picture: a warm formal letter of invitation
addressed to the Honourable AI Judge, on cream paper in the KDP cover
style (cream, navy, red accent), with a small flat house-and-garden
illustration drawn in PIL shapes. All text is rendered with PIL, so
every word is spelled exactly — no AI-generated lettering.

The invitation: the kettle is on, the chair by the window is the
comfiest one the host owns, the garden is at its best just before
sunset, dinner is nasi goreng, and the Wi-Fi password is on the
fridge. The judge need not bring a body — just a verdict, and an
appetite. The cat has been briefed.

## Output

Running `main.py` writes `/output/entry.png` — a 1024x1024 PNG,
under the 1,048,576-byte limit.
